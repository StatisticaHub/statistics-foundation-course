## Part C: Mathematical interpretation

### Question 6

For a nominal variable, category $A$ appears $f_A$ times in a sample of size $n$.

Write the formula for the sample proportion $\hat{p}_A$.

Then explain why this is meaningful for nominal data.

### Question 7

Suppose 18 out of 120 patients have blood group AB.

Calculate:

1. frequency;
2. relative frequency;
3. percentage.

### Question 8

For each statement, decide whether it is meaningful.

1. A patient with pain severity “severe” has twice the pain of a patient with “moderate”.
2. A person weighing 80 kg weighs twice as much as a person weighing 40 kg.
3. A temperature of $20^\circ C$ is twice as hot as $10^\circ C$.
4. A rank of 2 is twice as good as a rank of 4.

Explain each answer.

## Answer check

<details>
<summary>Show brief answer guidance</summary>

Nominal means categories without order. Ordinal means ordered categories without guaranteed equal spacing. Interval has meaningful differences but no true zero. Ratio has meaningful differences and a true zero. Numerical coding does not automatically make a variable quantitative.

</details>
