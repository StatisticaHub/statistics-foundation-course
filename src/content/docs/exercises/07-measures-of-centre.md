## Part A: Basic calculation

### Question 1

For the dataset:

$$
4,\ 6,\ 8,\ 10,\ 12
$$

calculate:

1. mean;
2. median;
3. mode.

### Question 2

For the dataset:

$$
4,\ 6,\ 8,\ 10,\ 100
$$

calculate:

1. mean;
2. median;
3. explain which better represents a typical value.

### Question 3

For the dataset:

$$
3,\ 3,\ 4,\ 5,\ 7,\ 7,\ 7,\ 9
$$

calculate:

1. mean;
2. median;
3. mode.

## Part B: Mean as balance point

### Question 4

For the dataset:

$$
2,\ 4,\ 6,\ 8
$$

1. Calculate $\bar{x}$.
2. Calculate each deviation $x_i-\bar{x}$.
3. Show that the deviations sum to zero.

### Question 5

Prove generally that:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar{x})=0.
$$

## Part C: Interpretation

### Question 6

A country reports average income using the mean.

Explain why the median income may be more informative for describing a typical household.

### Question 7

A class has marks:

$$
20,\ 22,\ 25,\ 26,\ 27,\ 29,\ 90.
$$

1. Calculate the mean.
2. Calculate the median.
3. Explain how the outlier affects the mean.

### Question 8

A variable is nominal.

Explain why the mode may be meaningful but the mean and median may not be.

## Part D: Challenge problems

### Question 9

Create a dataset of 7 values where:

$$
\text{mean} > \text{median}.
$$

Explain what this suggests about the shape of the distribution.

### Question 10

Create a dataset of 7 values where:

$$
\text{mean} < \text{median}.
$$

Explain what this suggests about the shape of the distribution.

### Question 11

Find a dataset of 5 positive values where the mean is exactly 10 but the median is 6.

## Part E: Critical reasoning

### Question 12

A report gives only the mean and no measure of spread.

Explain why this is incomplete.

### Question 13

Can two datasets have the same mean but very different interpretations?

Construct an example and explain.

## Answer check

<details>
<summary>Show brief answer guidance</summary>

The mean uses all values and is sensitive to extreme observations. The median is based on order and is more resistant to outliers. The mode identifies the most frequent value or category.

</details>
