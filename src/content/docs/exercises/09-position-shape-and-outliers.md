## Part C: Outlier detection

### Question 7

For the dataset:

$$
4,\ 5,\ 6,\ 7,\ 8,\ 9,\ 10,\ 30
$$

1. Find $Q_1$ and $Q_3$.
2. Calculate the IQR.
3. Calculate the lower and upper outlier fences.
4. Determine whether 30 is flagged as a possible outlier.

### Question 8

## Answer check

<details>
<summary>Show brief answer guidance</summary>

Quartiles divide ordered data into four parts. The IQR is $Q_3-Q_1$. Right-skewed data have a long right tail and often mean greater than median. Outliers should be investigated before removal.
