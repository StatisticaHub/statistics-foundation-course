A theoretical and beginner-friendly foundation course in statistics.

This course is designed for students who want to build a strong mathematical foundation before studying biostatistics, epidemiology, data science, machine learning, or research methods.

> **Main idea:** Statistics is the science of learning from data under uncertainty.

## Course structure

| Module | Theme | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Foundations of Statistics | Data, variables, populations, samples, uncertainty |
| Module 2 | Descriptive Statistics | Centre, spread, shape, standardisation |
| Module 3 | Probability Foundations | Events, probability rules, conditional probability, Bayes theorem |
| Module 4 | Random Variables and Distributions | Expectation, variance, common distributions |
| Module 5 | Statistical Inference | Estimation, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing |

## Mathematical notation

Inline notation should look clean: $\mu$, $\bar{x}$, $X \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$.

Display notation should render properly:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i.
$$

Another example:

$$
\mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathcal{P}.
$$
