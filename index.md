# Statistics Foundation

A theoretical and beginner-friendly foundation course in statistics.

This course is designed for students who want to build a strong mathematical foundation before studying biostatistics, data science, machine learning, epidemiology, or research methods.

The course avoids programming and focuses on statistical reasoning, mathematical notation, derivations, and interpretation.

---

## Course structure

The course is organised into five modules.

| Module | Theme | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Foundations of Statistics | Data, variables, populations, samples, uncertainty |
| Module 2 | Descriptive Statistics | Tables, graphs, centre, spread, standardisation |
| Module 3 | Probability Foundations | Events, probability rules, conditional probability, Bayes theorem |
| Module 4 | Random Variables and Distributions | Random variables, expectation, variance, distributions |
| Module 5 | Statistical Inference | Sampling distributions, estimation, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing |

---

## Course style

Lectures are written as character-based classroom conversations.

Notes are descriptive, theoretical, and mathematically detailed.

Exercises are designed to test statistical understanding without coding.
