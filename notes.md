# Notes

This section contains detailed theoretical notes.

The notes are more formal than the lectures and include definitions, mathematical notation, equations, derivations, assumptions, interpretation, and examples.

No coding is used in this course.

---

## Notes format

Each note follows this structure:

1. Learning objectives.
2. Formal definitions.
3. Mathematical notation.
4. Derivations where relevant.
5. Interpretation.
6. Worked theoretical examples.
7. Common mistakes.
8. Summary.
