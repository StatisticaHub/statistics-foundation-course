# About

Statistics Foundation is a StatisticaHub course created to help students build a strong theoretical foundation in statistics.

The course is designed for learners who want to study statistics seriously before moving into:

- mathematical statistics;
- biostatistics;
- epidemiology;
- data science;
- machine learning;
- research methods.

---

## Teaching approach

The course uses:

- character-based lectures;
- descriptive theoretical notes;
- mathematical notation;
- derivations;
- interpretation-focused exercises;
- no coding.

---
