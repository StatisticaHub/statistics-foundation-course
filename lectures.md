# Lectures

This section contains character-based conversational lectures.

The lectures introduce statistical ideas through classroom-style discussion between Mr. R and four students: Oliver, Emma, James, and Sophia.

The purpose of the lectures is to build intuition before moving into the formal notes.

---

## Lecture format

Each lecture follows this structure:

1. Opening scene.
2. Today's big question.
3. Intuition-first explanation.
4. Conversation between the characters.
5. Mathematical bridge.
6. Common mistake.
7. Key takeaway.
8. Reflection question.
