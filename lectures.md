## Lecture format

Each lecture uses the same five recurring characters:

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| Mr. R | Teacher and mentor |
| Oliver | Curious beginner |
| Emma | Careful and methodical student |
| James | Practical student |
| Sophia | Mathematically strong student |

The lectures are written as classroom conversations.

Each lecture follows this structure:

1. A short classroom scene.
2. A motivating question.
3. A conversation between Mr. R and the students.
4. Simple examples.
5. Misconceptions and corrections.
6. A gentle mathematical bridge.
7. A final summary.
